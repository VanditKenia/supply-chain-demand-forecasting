# Supply Intelligence Design System

## Product character

Modern logistics SaaS / operations control center. The experience must feel like software, not a presentation dashboard.

## Visual principles

- Warm neutral canvas rather than default Power BI white.
- Charcoal navigation and typography.
- Burnt orange / terracotta as the primary action accent.
- Muted olive for healthy status.
- Amber for attention.
- Brick red for critical risk.
- Sparse borders and strong typography.
- Asymmetric composition.
- Progressive disclosure.
- Motion communicates state.

## Interaction principles

- Persistent navigation.
- Filter drawer instead of permanent slicer walls.
- Rich analytical hover states.
- Click-to-context interactions.
- Detail drawer for decision units.
- "Why?" explanations for replenishment recommendations.
- Store and product exploration.
- 3D reserved for meaningful network exploration.

## Anti-patterns

Do not use:
- generic navy-blue BI templates
- rainbow charts
- excessive rounded cards
- decorative gauges
- fake AI insights
- fake real-time indicators
- unexplained metrics
- animation without a functional purpose
